Q&A

Crumbs of curiosity

Is Kairo in stores?

Not yet. This is a concept website, so nothing here is for sale and no orders are taken.

How is the 3D made?

Every cookie is built in code from a lathe-turned wafer, a lumpy cream layer and your Kairo logo wrapped on the face, lit by a hand-written WebGL2 shader. No 3D models are loaded.

Can I add more flavors?

Yes. Flavors, colours, names and prices live in one list at the top of the script, so a new flavor only needs a new line.

Does the shop charge anything?

No. The box and basket are a front-end demo. Nothing is sold and no payment details are collected.
